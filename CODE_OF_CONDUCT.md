# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, ability, or experience level.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, constructive, and specific. Assume good intent while addressing impact. Accept feedback gracefully. Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people. Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces when someone is representing LANORA. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they consider harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's repository contact/reporting options. Maintainers will review reports as promptly and confidentially as possible. This code of conduct is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/ .
